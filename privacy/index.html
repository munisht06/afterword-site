Privacy notice

Privacy, in plain language.

Afterword is a private place for friends reading together. This notice explains what the Afterword app collects, who can see it and how to remove it.

Information the service uses

  • Your account email and authentication records, managed through Supabase Auth.
  • Your reader name, club memberships, invitations and host role.
  • Books, reading plans, discussions, replies, lounge messages and reactions you contribute.
  • Your private chapter progress, saved discussions, reveal permissions and paper preference.
  • Private blocks and reports, including the content and context attached to a report, and content-free records of moderation actions.

Afterword has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We do not sell your information or use it to profile you.

Who can see it

Current club members can see their club’s conversations, subject to chapter permissions and blocks. Your chapter progress and saved discussions are visible only to you. Hosts manage books, invitations and membership; hosting does not unlock chapters or reveal safety reports. Only the Afterword operator can review safety reports.

Keeping the community safe

To keep clubs welcoming, Afterword automatically checks new discussions, replies, lounge messages, reader names, reading prompts and club names against a short list of slurs and abusive terms, and asks you to edit text that contains them. The check runs on our server when you post; it does not read your messages for any other purpose. You can report or block a reader from any discussion, reply or message.

Providers and book search

Supabase provides authentication, the database and server functions, hosted in the United States (US East). Resend delivers account emails. Open Library supplies public book information; book searches send your search words to Open Library. Apple distributes the app. Private club conversations are not used to train or generate AI content. This website has no advertising or analytics scripts.

Storage and security

On your phone, your sign-in session is kept in the platform’s secure storage. Club access and chapter permissions are enforced by our server, not just the app. No system is perfectly secure, and we will update this notice if our practices change.

Keeping and deleting information

Club history stays until the content or club is removed. Leaving a club ends your access but does not delete what you wrote. Deleting your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) removes your identity, sessions, conversations, memberships, private progress and preferences, and reports that contain your content. Clubs you host are deleted with their history; replies by others to your deleted discussions are removed with them. Copies that other people saved or shared independently cannot be erased by Afterword.

Afterword currently runs on Supabase’s free plan, which does not include point-in-time recovery or separate off-site backups. Deleted information is therefore not kept in an Afterword backup, but it also means data can be lost in a provider failure. Supabase may keep short-lived operational logs under its own policies.

Children

Afterword is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.
